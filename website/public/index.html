Canonical evidence

Keep the truth before the projection.

Analytics destinations are lossy by design. ETLayer stores policy-clean canonical evidence first, so replay, revalidation, inspection, and migration remain possible later.

01

Trusted provenance
Identity is established at the ingest boundary, not accepted from payload claims.

02

Explicit authority
A producer may submit a valid event and still be forbidden to assert it.

03

Replayable history
Policy decisions and event evidence remain inspectable and append-only.

Semantic model

Separate the dimensions
vendors usually collapse.

ETLayer keeps identity, authority, tenancy and causality explicit so automation can reason from evidence instead of conventions.

subject

Who the product journey is about.

actor

Who or what directly acted.

delegation

On whose behalf the action occurred.

producer

Which authenticated system supplied the evidence.

authority

What that producer is allowed to assert.

provenance

Trusted identity established by ingest.

causation

The direct causal predecessor.

correlation

The larger activity or journey.

External integration contract

Your app only needs OTLP.

Send standard OpenTelemetry events with an issued project credential. ETLayer owns policy, evidence and routing behind the boundary.

OTLP / HTTP 
curl https://<your-etlayer-ingest>/v1/logs \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ETLAYER_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  --data @otlp-event.json
